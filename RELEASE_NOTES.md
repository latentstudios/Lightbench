## What's new
- **AI works offline.** On first launch Lightbench offers to download all AI features (about 600 MB) in the background. After that, masking, search, tagging and AI Denoise work with no internet. Manage them in *Lightbench menu → AI Models*.
- **Faster AI.** AI now uses all your CPU cores, so smart search, tagging and click-to-select load up to 5× faster. Opening Masking preloads the one-click mask models, so the first click doesn't have to wait. AI Denoise is about 30% faster on most graphics cards.
- **Rotate radial gradients** with a new rotation knob (or the Angle slider). Hold Shift to snap to 15°.
- Everything Lightbench needs is now built into the app — no code is downloaded while you work.
- **Update notifications**, **Send Feedback / Report a Problem** and a **Welcome guide** (Lightbench menu, top-left).

## Fixed
- Mac: Lightbench no longer shows up as "damaged and can't be opened".
- Version 1.0.1 could fail to start with "Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'publish')". Install this version over it — your catalog and edits are kept.

## Installing
- **Windows:** download `Lightbench-Setup-win-x64.exe` and run it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click *More info → Run anyway*.
- **Mac:** download the `.dmg` for your Mac (Apple silicon or Intel) and drag Lightbench into Applications. The first time, open it, click *Done*, then go to *System Settings → Privacy & Security* and click *Open Anyway*.
